# Grouped prototype screw interfaces

All coordinates and nominal grip/engagement values are exported in [joint_coordinates.csv](../export/2026-11-08_grouped/2026-11-08_joint_coordinates.csv); they come from the same tables that create holes and screw references.

- Each bladder dry clamp: four M2.5×10 at local (±36, ±36), recessed heads; nominal 7.65 mm engagement.
- Each cup to chassis: four M2.5×10 at (±32, ±34), 3 mm grip / 7 mm engagement, driven from below through Ø6.4 column bores.
- Each moving cap: two M2.5×6 guide-stop screws and two OD8 × 1 mm washers. These stop travel; no fixed screw locks the cap to the chassis.
- Seventeen shared section splice plates: four screws each. The 15 low splices have Ø6 × 2.25 mm head pockets on the inside of the bottom panels. Install the two elevated service-centre splices before the pumps and G1 hose.
- The PCB tray stack uses four M2.5×10 screws through Ø2.7 lower-tray holes into upper-leg pilots. Ø6 head pockets clear the neighbouring lower feet. Preassemble this stack before mounting it to the panel.
- The eight bottom panels are driven from below. Both service covers are driven from above with four M2.5×8 screws per cover. The heads sit 0.15 mm below the 73 mm plane in the nominal CAD. Trays, cradles, valve carrier and six tube clips screw to the panels; each tube clip uses one outboard screw.

M2.5 clearance holes are Ø2.7; thread-forming pilots Ø2.1. These are material-dependent starting dimensions, not a screw torque specification. Validate the coupon before full printing. Heat-set inserts require a different receiving pocket. Keep the panel-off tool access and the stated assembly sequence.
