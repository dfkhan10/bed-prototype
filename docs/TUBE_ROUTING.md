# Tube routing

The current six-pump plan uses three pumps feeding the common pressure line and three connected to the common extraction line. The chassis reserves four positions per bank; keep each unused header connection sealed. Installed pump roles and parallel-bank suitability require testing with the actual parts. The two lines each serve both groups through separate normally closed two-way valves, four valves in total. At each group's service tee, one port goes to the fill valve, one to the extraction valve, and one to that group's trunk. To hold a group, close both valves. One group can inflate while the other deflates. Three available valves do not complete the specified two-group circuit.

| Group | Pair in row 1 | Pair in row 2 | Pair in row 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | A1 and C1 | B2 and D2 | A3 and C3 |
| G2 | B1 and D1 | A2 and C2 | B3 and D3 |

Rows 1 and 2 use a four-port cross, with one port for each cell, one incoming trunk and one continuing trunk. Row 3 uses a terminal tee with no onward port. Each cell uses one bidirectional hose. The two cells in a pair share the group line and are not controlled separately.

The CAD uses nominal 4 mm ID, 6 mm OD and a 12 mm bend radius. Connector bodies, pump port locations, and hose compliance need physical verification. Hose lengths in the cut list follow nominal CAD centrelines and include spans to fitting centres. Measure the lengths between actual seats and add removal allowance when cutting. Confirm which nozzle is the inlet and which is the outlet. The second port on each pump is an ambient-side geometric allowance. Check clearance and ventilation with the actual pump head, hose, wire exits and any ambient-side filters or mufflers.

Reserve passages for later services are 16 mm wide × 13 mm high, from z35 to z48, above the main cell hose runs. The viewer's routing mode shows the four longitudinal passages and three transverse passages. They are clearance volumes and are not printed. Use the removable underside panels for access. Check that new cables or hoses do not pull on the TPU ports or prevent the panels from being removed.
