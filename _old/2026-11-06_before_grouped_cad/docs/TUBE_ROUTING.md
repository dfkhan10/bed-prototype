# Tube routing: A1–C4

One independent 4 mm ID / 6 mm OD hose per cell. A selectable group is created by valve states; cell hoses do not join an uncontrolled common tee upstream of their isolation valves.

- Twelve Ø6.5 ports pass through the **2 mm floors** beneath the **2 mm cell seats**.
- The open underside provides a 32 mm-high routing space; 4 mm ribs support the roof between cells.
- Three **34 × 12 mm** bundle tunnels cross the transverse ribs and the cell/service bulkhead. Each column carries four hoses; a 30 mm-wide array of four Ø6 hoses at 8 mm centres fits nominally inside 34 mm.
- Nominal route level is Z=16 mm. Cell routes travel toward **−Y to `mate_y`**. Valve-side routing starts at the service side of that plane and continues into the **same open cavity**. The old reversed-Y blind stub grooves were removed.
- At pitch 76 the same windows align across the extra cell-deck seam.

The model reserves routing volume; it does not claim collision-checked hose centre-lines, individual valve ports, clip retention or fitting bend radii. Keep the four hoses separated and labelled at both ends. Add clips/strain relief after actual hose and barb tests. The 0.3 mm seam gap must not pinch a hose.

Valve references are 22 × 12 × 30 mm and are provisional. Confirm actual port direction, barb length, minimum hose bend radius, valve state and exhaust arrangement before finalizing mounts or cell-to-valve hose lengths.

Selectable cells may share one pressure source. Pump count follows air demand and inflation time at pressure, rather than the count of valves. A shared manifold also couples the pressure of simultaneously open cells; independent target pressures require a suitable control strategy and isolation.
