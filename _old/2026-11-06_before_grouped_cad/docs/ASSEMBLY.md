# Assembly: Fusion research deck

Current grid: A–C left to right, rows 1–4 from head (+Y) to foot/service (−Y). Current pitch: 60 mm. The printed set contains four `BedFrame_*` STLs; valve/screw reference STLs are excluded.

1. Test an M2.5 × 10 thread-forming screw in a Ø2.1 pilot coupon. Inspect material splitting and strip-out. Do not substitute an insert without changing the pilot/boss dimensions.
2. Check one cell cassette against the **56 mm opening, 2 mm recess, 2 mm floor**, and Ø6.5 port. The port is hidden by the installed cassette. Confirm barb seating and support under the thin floor.
3. Join the cell deck to the service cover with **4 horizontal M2.5 screws**. Align X/Z holes at the 0.3 mm butt seam. Insert from inside the open cell deck toward −Y. Use a short hex driver and tighten before routing. At pitch 76, first join the two cell-deck modules with the four additional seam screws.
4. Trial-fit the 12 valves under the service roof, in the 4×3 guide layout. The reference bodies are estimates. Confirm actual fittings, retention and wire access before drilling or printing a final revision.
5. Route one independent hose per cell through the open rib tunnels and three bulkhead windows. Each window accommodates a four-hose column bundle. Keep all hoses below the seat floors; test bends with actual fittings. Follow [TUBE_ROUTING](TUBE_ROUTING.md).
6. With the bottom panel **off**, place the bay below the service cover. Insert **8 M2.5 screws upward** through the bay's 4 mm upper flange into the service receiving bosses. Heads face the bay cavity; reach them from below.
7. Pump/manifold and controller installation is pending the actual kit. There is deliberately no fixed pump tray, controller hole pattern or assumed exhaust circuit.
8. Fit the full bottom panel with **8 M2.5 screws upward** into the bay's lower blind bosses. Nominal grip 4 mm and engagement 6 mm. The panel can later be removed without disturbing the upper bay screws.
9. Install cassettes without screws through air chambers. Pneumatic commissioning follows confirmation of the valve type, pressure limit and exhaust path; then start with one unloaded cell and check leaks before testing groups.

The exploded file contains generic valve envelopes and generic screw references. Tubing, bladders, manifold, pumps, power entry and wiring are not installed geometry. Do not treat the enclosure as a pressure vessel or load-qualified structure.
