# Bed prototype

Open the [current Fusion master](export/2026-11-08_grouped/2026-11-08_GroupedBed.f3d) or the [offline review](export/2026-11-08_grouped/2026-11-08_design_review.html). The soft-surface version uses the 4 × 3 checkerboard field, an enclosure of 344 × 432 × 73 mm and 25 mm nominal rise above the common lowered datum. The optional caps have a separate print folder.

Start with the [one-cell STL set](export/2026-11-08_grouped/coupon/stl/) and the [assembly instructions](docs/ASSEMBLY.md). Fit the actual pumps, valves, connectors and tubing before printing the entire prototype.
